## Implementation

- Imported the 28 Towns source files from the local add-on; module registered after its NPC/Patrols/POI/HUD providers. Native ModuleHost handles enable/disable and save/load/world lifecycle. New-world reset uses only the existing SaveBus state event. Editable names and save paths stay under config/WildWasteland/Towns. First merged config binding imports existing add-on values only when their WW keys were absent. No live cfg/save/PNG edits by the agent.
- Faction is a candidate until the native CreateObject hook counts five actual matching faction actors. Human ownership excludes Yard Hounds; sparse human settlements remain white ruins. Native common loot/population totals, including an optional Camp_10's ordinary groups, share one balanced town roll. Shopkeepers/paid shop stock retain native layout/counts and enable after Coyote ownership. Camp_10 is optional only when its existing bake and outward entrance are compatible. Old save ownership uses native completed counters, conservatively deducting a possible hound.
- New layouts have no parking gap in the packed building rows. An open 24 x 40 m reservation sits outside the building cluster. Old towns move only their bare parking reservation, retaining building transforms; invisible walking links are recomputed. There is no slab or internal road painting. A short exterior ground approach is graded without artwork when original height/POI checks allow it. Cities without a certified approach retain their buildings and initial bus but are deferred as travel destinations.
- Buildings start on main-tile apply, nearest unfinished town first; at most one indivisible prefab per frame, separated by 40 ms. Two attempts per failed lot and eight seconds for map preparation; no play/loading/UI gate. Native saves suspend new construction, pre-spawn starts and traffic changes.
- Each owned Coyote town seeds one ordinary merchant bus (stable shop-type selection), saved once rather than replenished when it leaves. A bus waits a random 600–900 game seconds, then requests another real Coyote-town destination. Its stock/health/crew use the existing traffic save and template builder. Existing merchant enable/cap/unique travelling shop-type controls apply to departures. Parking reservations prevent two visitors choosing the same bay. No valid destination/certified route means it waits and retries instead of disappearing or inventing an endpoint. Arrived buses release travelling slots; seeded and visitor bays are distinct.
- Convoys use real owned human towns and certified native-road routes. Arrival releases the convoy limit immediately. Crew transfer through native BailOut with identity/health preserved, one person per traffic tick and driver last. This does not depend on Return to car. Truck remains; escort cars are removed only after their living crew is out. An unseen arrival materializes the truck and its recorded occupants when approached. Parked guard names persist in traffic JSON and reconnect to native saved NPCs. A player-taken truck is released from parking control and cleanup preserves hired guards.
- Merchants stay physical while in the player's native unzoomed map footprint; unload only after 120 seconds outside it, retaining their world record. Parked convoys expire after a random 600–900 seconds continuously outside that footprint. Native far-item cleanup is held by the existing guarded retention hook until managed retirement. No second scheduler or independent Update loop was introduced by Towns.
- TownStore6 / generator9 / new-layout8 / paths3 keeps readers for earlier formats. Adds actual population count/measurement and one-time merchant seed state. Existing traffic JSON has additive optional fields for town destination, visit, parked/seed bus and guard names; existing old readers ignore those keys. Old standalone DLLs cannot read the new Towns binary format. TownStore retains its previous-file .bak publication behavior.

## Saved town restoration follow-up — 2026-10-20, Testing

Denis loaded `merchantnearcity` (`WW_save-03a10ee6d5d848dcbbe098efc1f5c3e9.es3`) and found the city/inhabitants absent, with saved loot on the ground. Read-only inspection confirms its Towns sidecar still contains all 18 layouts and their spawner states; High Spur is at its saved world position (-8208.423, 0.02918, 8831.783). The log after this load builds only the initial 0/0 town, while native loot and traffic restore.

Cause: native `TerrainTile.Move` calls `Dist`, which starts generation and invokes `OnBeforeTilePrepare`, **before** raising `OnTileMoved` (Sources/code/MapMagic/MapMagic.Terrains/TerrainTile.cs:391–423). Towns' move callback then unconditionally removed those fresh captures. Finalized/Applied could no longer find them, so moved terrain tiles received no town geometry. `ForgetMoved` now preserves captures matching the current epoch and tile coordinate, including their POI policy, and cancels only stale work. Saved plans remain authoritative; no replacement layout or actor population is invented.
