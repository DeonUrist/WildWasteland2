# Automatic skill perks — 2026-10-23 — Testing

Denis approved the full multi-module/Core scope exception for this feature. Existing skills had numeric bonuses and ten insanity perks; the 36 automatic threshold perks had no gameplay hooks. This implementation derives ownership from the existing skill values at 25/50/75/100, keeps the numeric bonuses and insanity choices, and adds no Crafting or Medicine perks. No new configuration entries, renamed identifiers, commits or pushes.

The 36 definitions are in `src/Modules/Perks/SkillPerkRoster.cs`. The TAB Skills grid shows all 36 skill perks in skill-list order and ascending thresholds, followed by all ten insanity perks in a separate row. Locked perks have gray borders and cached grayscale artwork; hover descriptions show requirements and current skill level. Skill rows use 20-point text at 24-unit spacing, leaving three rows of 54-unit skill tiles and a separate insanity row within the existing background. The description panel ends at 830 inside the background's 852 bottom edge, and long descriptions use smaller text. An earned perk queues an icon/name above the screen centre, plays a short chime, rises and fades over three seconds. Paused/editor sessions defer the presentation; loading an existing qualifying skill grants ownership without replaying notices. The same notice also presents newly earned insanity perks.

Named, valid transparent PNG placeholders are under `src/Modules/Perks/Embedded/Icons/`. The build installs missing defaults under `E:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\Apocalypter\BepInEx\plugins\WildWasteland\Perks\Icons\`, preserving customized icons. Replace those installed files with artwork and restart. Barter 50 uses `Salesman.png`; Barter 100 uses `Master Trader.png`.

The supplied `lvlup.wav` is packaged under `src/Modules/Perks/Embedded/Sounds/` and installed as `Perks/Sounds/lvlup.wav`. It replaces the synthesized chime and also plays on ordinary skill-level gains, including developer boosts. A level gain and its perk unlock share one playback; simultaneous gains do not overlap the sound. The sound can finish after the three-second icon fades. Loading existing skill values stays silent.

## Implementation

- Gunplay applies criticals, full-health/unaware multipliers and executions at the native final Health subtraction. Refreshing bleeding uses the native Bodypart/Damage path with a secondary-damage guard. Shotgun pellets share a blast collector; damage is applied once per victim after all pellets finish, with Crowd Control using the combined total and one Big Blast/Boomstick roll per victim. Unperked shotguns retain the ordinary path.
- Native Attack waits, reload Animator playback, pump EffectPlay waits and third-person reload/pump presentation carry speed effects. Refunds write only the current magazine and read its native capacity checks when the weapon catalog has no capacity. Adrenaline refreshes a five-second clock. Marksman adds 25% spread reduction while aiming; Run & Gun adds 25% while moving; Long Shot applies to rifles/snipers/crossbows. Urban Warrior uses the existing direction arrows in yellow, within 200 m for five seconds.
- NPC reactions use FsmPause, existing brain/idle suspension, native dead-prefab rigidbodies/colliders/joints on the living skeleton, and native Bodypart damage for the temporary body colliders. Recovery restores the actor and uses the existing human GetUp1/2/3 or canine GetUp clips. Human recovery chooses the closest initial chest orientation from the actual three clips. The user's later restriction takes precedence: mutants lacking custom recovery animations are never knocked down; shotgun push remains available. Full-health/execute thresholds use prefab health with existing boss scaling. Contact sets prevent continuous sprint collision from repeating Paintrain.
- Undertaker uses the chainsaw witness/cover mechanic. Enhanced chainsaw fear requests crouching at the destination for 12 seconds; ordinary melee fear retains six seconds. Weapon kills are deduplicated across damage, bleeding, chainsaw and corpse callbacks.
- Friendly Face halves the native Coyote hostility wait while preserving immediate retaliation. Charismatic halves the common hire-price function used for display and payment. Salesman selects the highest-value eligible item in vehicle cargo, using the native buy/sell eligibility list and existing ItemName/SellValue. It follows the last vehicle; an old save without that identity falls back to a nearby non-patrol vehicle.
- Merchant dialogue appends the two requested choices through the current quest/native or external dialogue adapter. Each use is stored independently in `WildWasteland.Perks.Merchants.v1` in the existing ES3 transaction. Moving merchants retain an optional stable identity in their existing record. Rerolls replace unsold stock, keep purchased items, and retain merchant guards and other state. A save attempted while native stock/wanted generation is unfinished reports a SaveBus failure instead of recording partial inventory; close dialogue and allow generation to finish before saving.
- Clean Cut rolls once per animal carcass, and once for human butchering when Cannibal permits it. Cooking records now have a backward-compatible version 2 carrying the final insanity reduction; version 1 records still load. Fasting changes native Hunger/Thirst drain actions. Strong Mind applies the requested additive +50/+50/+50 with 100 caps to Persevere.

## Denis's in-game checklist

Use a disposable save, and keep the installed MD5 matched to the handover before reading logs. F7 Developer > Perks has the existing +5 buttons for reaching thresholds. Close the editor after each increase so normal game time resumes. Optional global VerboseLog enables existing per-hit/extra-meat detail.

1. **Unlock/UI/load:** stop at 20, 45, 70 and 95 in each of the nine skills; add five, close the editor and expect exactly one chime/name/icon rising above the crosshair. TAB > Skills must show the unlocked icon and threshold/description. Save/reload above each threshold: effects and icons remain, with no replayed chime. Load an older qualifying save and check the same. Crafting/Medicine keep their old behavior.
2. **Unarmed:** kick at 25 and observe backward movement. At 50, repeat punches/kicks against a humanoid/rat/quadruped to observe occasional stun. At 75, compare first full-health hit with a subsequent hit (2x perk multiplier only on the full-health hit); eligible bosses/large mutants can stun at 20%. At 100, supported rigs ragdoll for 2-3 seconds then play a getup; they resume combat. Unsupported mutants stay upright. Hit a knocked-down arm/torso and verify body damage rather than a headshot.
3. **Melee:** at 25, one cutting hit gives exactly three one-second bleed ticks, each one third of the hit; another hit restarts three ticks rather than adding a second bleed. Blunt hits sometimes stun. At 50, hold a melee weapon while an enemy uses melee: parries cause no health or armor loss; gunshots still hit. At 75, hit an enemy already at <=25% max HP and expect execution; one just above that boundary should survive ordinary damage if sufficient health remains. At 100, a melee kill frightens witnesses once; a chainsaw kill adds destination crouching for 12 seconds, followed by normal behavior.
4. **Pistols:** compare reload at 24/25 and shot interval at 74/75; both mechanics and animation must speed up by 1.5x. At 50, repeated body/head hits should produce occasional 2x critical damage (25%/50%, respectively). At 100, supported small/humanoid/quadruped headshots knock down; supported large mutants roll 50% and juggernauts 10%. The later no-custom-animation restriction still applies.
5. **SMGs:** at 25, compare spread while moving/stationary. At 50, compare damage close up and at effective range (1.5x smoothly down to 1x). At 75, successful hits occasionally add one round to the currently held magazine without reserve changes or overfill; switch weapons before a projectile arrives and verify no refund into the wrong gun. At 100, kill an enemy: fire/reload speed becomes 1.5x for five gameplay seconds; another kill refreshes, never stacks; wait and confirm normal speed returns.
6. **Rifles/crossbows:** check additional aimed spread reduction at 25 and 25% range extension at 50. At 75, a first unaware hit is 3x, while subsequent aware hits are normal. At 100, a kill shows yellow arrows for registered enemies within 200 m for five seconds, reusing the direction indicator presentation. Check both rifle and crossbow.
7. **Shotguns:** at 25, observe push and occasional stun, with one roll per victim per blast. At 50, pumping/reloading and their animations are 1.5x. At 75, arrange two enemies: if four pellets hit A and three hit B at final 10 damage each, expect 70 on each, rather than repeated pellet application. A blast hitting only A retains the ordinary pellet sum. At 100, supported enemies use ragdoll instead of stun and the probability uses twice the final blast damage / pre-hit HP. Repeat with player projectiles enabled and disabled.
8. **Athletics:** at 25, compare ladder/ledge traversal with the prior level (1.5x). At 50, compare incoming damage while moving and stationary (0.75x while moving). At 75, sprint into a humanoid, remain in continuous contact through recovery, and verify no repeated knockdown until separating and colliding again. At 100, sprint backward and sideways; speed/stamina still follow the normal running state and the existing Athletics bonus.
9. **Barter:** at 25, attacking Coyotes still causes retaliation, with half the usual hostility wait. At 50, place a valuable eligible item in the car's locked cargo and visit a merchant: its name and actual sell value must appear in wanted items. Empty/invalid cargo must leave a valid board. At 75, compare hire display and charged price (half). At 100, buy one item, use the stock option and ensure the purchased item stays yours with no duplicates. Use wanted reroll separately; both options disappear independently. Let generation finish, save/reload, and confirm both uses stay consumed and merchant state remains intact. Repeat for stationary and travelling merchants, another merchant, and another save slot.
10. **Survival:** at 25, repeated butchering occasionally yields exactly one extra meat. At 50, last-handle and cook food/stew, then compare final insanity gain (0.75x after existing food effects); save/load the cooked item and repeat. At 75, compare hunger/thirst increase over equal gameplay time (old drain / 1.5). At 100, choose Persevere with values below and near the cap: each becomes min(100, old + 50); without it, the original 100/100/80 remains.

Confirmation logs: `[Perks] Skill perk unlocked: <name> (<skill> <threshold>).`; `[Perks] Merchant reroll: <identity>; stock used.` or `wanted used.`; verbose `[Survival] Clean Cut: +1 <item>.`; `[Survival] Persevere: hunger=...; thirst=...; fatigue=...`. Existing Gunplay verbose damage/Health and NPC retreat traces remain available. Random chance perks require multiple trials and are not guaranteed to trigger in a short sequence.
