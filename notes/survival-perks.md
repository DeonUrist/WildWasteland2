Corrupt records fail saving rather than becoming empty progress.

Cooked: feeds 15, health +5, insanity +25. Cooking uses the native Fire/cooking replacement flow. Failed item creation removes partial loot and retains the corpse. Animal butchering keeps its native event.

Loading, scene changes and module shutdown release modal ownership. A later break presents the same three choices for now.
