## Implementation

The prior shader sampled a small atlas area and reduced it to one tint with limited brightness variation. This pass separates hurricane shading into HurricaneSand.shader; the approved wall/front rollers retain SmokeParticles unchanged. It reuses the existing 128³ R8 density texture; no new image generation or texture memory allocation. WeatherAssetBuild adds hurricane-sand.mat to the bundle and WeatherVisuals selects it.

WeatherVortexSmoke draws three nested coherent annular envelopes at .88/.99/1.10 radius. Independent angular rates .28/.306/.332 rad/s and upward rates .043/.047/.051 normalized heights/s are scaled by per-column variation. Each fragment integrates dust through local shell thickness, 4/6/8 steps for Low/Balanced/Max, two density scales and one approximate light-occlusion sample per step. Premultiplied transmission accumulation supplies softness and overlap instead of hard RGB surface highlights. Directional scattering, existing backlight controls and shadow attenuation supply color contrast. This is a bounded layered volume approximation, not fluid simulation or physically complete multiple scattering. Final normal uses a continuous profile derivative; triangle derivative normals in an intermediate preview exposed facets and were replaced before deployment.

Nested layers have separate ordered inward/outward materials, selected per layer from the camera position; six shared materials replace two and three draw submissions replace one per visible core. Mesh counts stay unchanged. Geometry adds low-frequency lobes, baked-density bulges and per-column sway. Widths cycle 1/.10/.72/.18/.92/.065/.56/.25; atmosphere eye radii follow width. Layout places this alternating broad/slender pattern from left to right with conservative radius budgets and at least 350 m between top envelopes; the count is capped to fit. Existing per-region count/readout/log report actual/requested. A single storm can place fewer than the requested density. All existing palette/config entries, wall, rollers, local sand, rocks and darkening retain their settings. No new settings, CPU particle loops, jobs or real-time lights.
