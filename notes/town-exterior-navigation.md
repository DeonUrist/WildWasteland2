Nav.TownGround is an instance-only detached grid spanning the complete town outline plus 4 m outward margin. It reads floor/blocked/open values from the already prepared building maps and probes only gaps, reusing Nav.Floor, the native body clearance/slope rules, BakeEdges, ComponentsStep and line-of-sight movement rules. Resolution is at least 1 m and adapts to roughly 40,000 cells for large sites. It never registers a replacement building map, writes .wwnav/PoiNav files, or changes authored ladders/climbs/roofs. TownNavigation prepares this grid in its existing startup slice before connecting portals. A portal can connect through the exterior component even when the direct street segment is obstructed; existing local building routing still owns the leg through the entrance, and the original street graph remains available.

Runtime exterior routes use at most eight shared goal-area distance fields per town, pooled native distance arrays/heaps, one-at-a-time LRU eviction and a 500 us scheduler job advancing at most 64 heap pops per operation. Actors sharing a destination reuse a field; the first caller waits at its current position while its needed portion is expanded. Waypoints are smoothed over the grid and validated with at most two capsule sweeps, keeping the first safe step if the long shortcut fails. Wall-adjacent cells receive the existing clearance cost. Floating-origin adjustments update the detached frame/heights; site/routing disposal returns cached buffers. No per-NPC ground baking, recurring scene scans or all-map rebuilding is introduced.
