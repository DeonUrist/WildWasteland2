## Behavior

- Existing vanilla and WW incoming-hit observations relay the actual victim/attacker through a typed Services event. Nearby damage to a seated or dismounted member updates its crew's threat. Native NPC melee reports this event without changing ordinary NPC hurt behavior. Same-car and same-faction attackers are excluded; attacking Coyotes still uses the existing hostility transition.
- Crew checks run at 2 Hz. A car below 1 m/s for one second, attacked within the past four seconds, exits if the enemy is within 3.5 m of an occupant or nobody can answer with a ranged shot (weapon, fire arc, range and unobstructed line). With a usable car, attackers already beyond 50 m are pursued by vehicle instead of causing exit/reboard loops.
- Combat exits use the existing door wait and native-prefab replacement, retaining current health, appearance and faction. They hold and protect the vehicle without marking it permanently abandoned or scheduling its ordinary vacancy explosion. Driver/passenger and turret membership remains available to reboard; a surviving member can replace a dead driver.
- Combat members reboard when their recorded attacker dies, is destroyed/unloaded, or exceeds 50 m from the vehicle, for both NPCs and the player. The typed NPC crew order suspends combat while the existing navigation walks them to their seats. If the threat returns inside 50 m, boarding pauses and foot members fight again while the vehicle stays held. A new attack can replace the recorded threat.
- Once aboard, surviving attackers are pursued using the same driving pilot; when the attacker is dead, the ordinary route/merchant wait resumes. Merchant parking yields only during this combat pursuit. Convoy and battle route membership is retained through the existing traffic hold/reattach path.
- Convoys now use the existing maximum-recovery bailout too. Fully stuck/out-of-fuel, flipped, wrecked, captured and unusable cars retain their return exclusions. Reaching maximum recovery marks the car fully stuck even if its configured bailout roll elects to wait; actual movement clears that provisional flag before abandonment.
- Merchant driver/passenger and human turret exits and boarding use the front Rustliner door. Exit positions are slightly separated outside that same door to avoid stacking bodies. Ordinary non-merchant buses retain their rear-door behavior. Passive non-merchant editor setups remain passive.
- Merchant human turrets use the native prefab maximum. Old amplified saved records are reduced proportionally, preserving percentage wounded (e.g. 200/400 becomes 50/100), while dead guards remain dead. The short post-spawn health restorer receives the normalized value too. Boarding preserves current wounds instead of replaying the pre-combat merchant record.

No new config keys, save formats, assets, hooks, scheduler jobs or version change. The existing Return to car description was updated. Return membership remains runtime-only as before; save/reload does not restore an in-progress regroup. Existing wiki work is preserved. No commit/push/release or standard-game deployment was requested.
