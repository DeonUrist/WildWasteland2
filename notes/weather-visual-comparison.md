This pass adds the original animated billows, multi-scale near dust and local sky treatment. It also replaces rising clumps with bounded baked-density vortex columns, deep placement, broad dusty feet and flared cloud-covered tops. These are authoring improvements, not evidence of movie equivalence or a performance win. Remaining priorities are full-front terrain fitting, transparency sorting, shelter/cab suppression, actual light scattering/illumination, and audio/debris.

Density uses `round(shared rounded polygon area in km² × configured hurricanes/km²)`. It sets stable model locations, not a quality-dependent visible count. Frustum/distance culling can make fewer locations visible from one view. GPU volume sampling costs more per shaded pixel than a simple billboard; tier limits and fewer front puffs constrain work but do not guarantee unchanged frame time.
