## Causes and changes

The former hurricane shader concentrated density in a filled smooth Gaussian column: little radial texture contrast, no eye, and old bound scale 850x1100x850 with a narrow .23 core radius. Its 8/12/18 evenly spaced box samples spent work in empty space and could expose coarse integration structure. Replace this filled field, rather than layering another surface on it. New bounds 1450x1600x1450 contain an annular 3D density field: roughly 1.2–2 km visible diameter, expanding into the canopy and an approximately 600 m diameter eye at ground level. Samples follow curved-shell intervals, skipping empty box/central space. Noise rotates around the axis at .55 rad/s, shears with height and advects upward; Cartesian periodic density has no atan2 UV seam. Low/Balanced/Max = 12/18/24 samples. Density and lighting variation are stronger, and distance haze no longer erases most of the surface contrast. The shader traces the same shell from inside, rather than drawing crossing image planes. One occupied-eye uniform softens regional haze inside that eye and reduces local dust. Source: VortexVolume.shader, Atmosphere.shader, WeatherVisuals.cs, WeatherAtmosphere.cs.

This is still bounded shader smoke, not fluid physics; in-game quality, transparency interactions and GPU cost require Denis's test. No claim of movie equivalence or zero performance loss.

The local-particle attempt was rejected in favor of vanilla cloud motion. Remove the previous custom ground/rising systems and their terrain probes.
