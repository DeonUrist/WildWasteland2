[size=5][b]What is Wild Wasteland?[/b][/size]

Wild Wasteland brings together the best gameplay mods for Apocalypter into [b]one single package[/b]. Everything was rebuilt to work together, so you get all the features with none of the clashes, and the game stays smooth even when the desert is full of raiders, gunfire and burning cars.

It is made of [b]modules[/b]. Each module is a big chunk of gameplay, and you can switch every module (and almost every feature inside it) on or off from the in-game settings. Want the smart enemies but not the new HUD? Turn the HUD off. Want a quiet trip without raider cars? Pick the [b]Loner[/b] setting. You are in control.

[size=5][b]PLAYER BODY & ANIMATION[/b][/size]
[i]You are not a floating camera anymore.[/i]

[list]
[*][b]A real body.[/b] Look down and see your legs, your torso and your shadow. Choose a female or male character.
[*][b]Third-person camera.[/b] Press the Change Camera key to see your character from over the shoulder, on foot or behind the wheel. Zoom with the mouse wheel, orbit around yourself, and aim with a crosshair that shows where your shots will really land.
[*][b]Full animations.[/b] Walking, running, sprinting, crouching, jumping, kicking, aiming, reloading one shell at a time, pumping a shotgun, throwing grenades and blast lances, swinging melee weapons. Every move is animated.
[*][b]Your gear on your back.[/b] Your backpack, rifles, pistols, blades, binoculars and flashlight hang on your body where they belong.
[*][b]Climbing.[/b] Push into a ledge, press Jump, and pull yourself up onto walls, rocks, crates and parked cars.
[*][b]You in the driver's seat.[/b] Your own character drives the car. Lean out and shoot, throw grenades or blast lances from the seat, turn the key in the ignition with one button.
[*][b]Smoother feel.[/b] Gentle weapon sway, eased aim-down-sights, and a proper ragdoll when you fall.

[i]Every bullet is real.[/i]

[size=5][b]BUILT TO RUN SMOOTH[/b][/size]

Apocalypter can slow down when mods fight over the same things. Wild Wasteland was built from the ground up so all of its parts share the work and stay out of each other's way. Heavy jobs are spread out over time so you do not feel them, and if one part ever runs into trouble, it switches itself off instead of ruining your game.

[size=5][b]INSTALLATION[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Install [b]BepInEx 5[/b] for Apocalypter. [b]Apocasetter[/b] is recommended for easy in-game settings.
[*]If you used any of the mods that Wild Wasteland includes (Apocaplayer, Apocaclimber, NPC Overhaul, Gunplay, Gunplay HUD, Apocaunloader, CleanHUD, Apocapatrol, War Rig), [b]remove them[/b] first. Your old settings are carried over automatically.
[*]Drop the Wild Wasteland folder into BepInEx\plugins.
[*]Start the game and hit the road.
[/list]

Your saves are safe. Saves from the old standalone mods load fine in Wild Wasteland.
